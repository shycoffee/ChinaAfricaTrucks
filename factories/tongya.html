About Tongya Heavy Industry

Tongya Heavy Industry (通亚重工) has been at the forefront of trailer manufacturing in China's "Trailer Capital" — Liangshan County, Shandong Province — since 2003. The company operates a modern production facility featuring intelligent robotic welding systems, automated sandblasting, and electrophoretic coating lines that ensure consistent, high-quality output.

Tongya's product range covers container transport semi-trailers, aluminum alloy powder tankers, bulk powder material transport trailers, and vertical powder tank semi-trailers. Each product is engineered for durability, safety, and compliance with international standards.

With a dedicated focus on the African market, Tongya builds trailers that withstand Africa's challenging road conditions — reinforced chassis, heavy-duty suspension, corrosion-resistant coatings, and tropical-grade components.
