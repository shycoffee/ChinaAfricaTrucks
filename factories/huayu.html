About Huayu Group

Founded in 2000, Huayu Group has grown from a small workshop into one of Liangshan's largest trailer manufacturers. The company occupies a modern production facility with advanced manufacturing equipment, automated welding systems, and professional engineering teams.

Huayu's product lineup covers the full spectrum of commercial trailers: flatbed semi-trailers for container transport, dump trailers for mining and construction, lowbed trailers for heavy equipment, and specialized bulk cargo trailers for agricultural and industrial use.

With a strong focus on the African market, Huayu builds trailers specifically engineered for African road conditions — reinforced chassis, heavy-duty suspension systems, and corrosion-resistant coatings for tropical climates.
