Manufacturers & Brands

Sinotruk HOWO

The HOWO T7H and T5G series are Africa's most popular tractor heads. Built on MAN TGA technology with Cummins engines and FAST transmissions. Known for reliability, parts availability across Africa, and excellent fuel economy. The 6x4 420HP HOWO is the best-selling tractor head in West Africa.

Shacman X3000

Shacman X3000 and X5000 series tractor heads feature MAN-derived cab design, Weichai WP13 engines (up to 540HP), and FAST 12-speed transmissions. The X3000 is prized for its spacious sleeper cab, smooth ride, and powerful engine braking — ideal for long-haul routes across Africa.

Dongfeng KL

Dongfeng's KL series offers competitive mid-range tractor heads with Yuchai engines. Excellent value for money with good fuel efficiency and reliable performance for regional transport operations.

Key Features

Proven Engines

Cummins ISG/ISX and Weichai WP series engines — the most trusted heavy-duty diesel engines in Africa. Millions of units in service, easy maintenance, available spare parts.

Fuel Efficient

Optimized drivetrain with FAST direct-drive transmissions and Cummins/Weichai common-rail injection. Typical fuel consumption: 28-33 L/100km loaded.

Driver Comfort

Sleeper cabins with A/C, air suspension driver and passenger seats, power windows, central locking, Bluetooth audio, and ample storage for long-haul operations.

Heavy-Duty Cooling

Oversized radiator, intercooler, and fan clutch system designed for tropical African temperatures. No overheating even in 45°C ambient conditions with full load.
