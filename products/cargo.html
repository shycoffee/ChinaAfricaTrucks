Manufacturers & Brands

Sinotruk (CNHTC)

China's largest heavy-duty truck manufacturer. Their HOWO series is the most popular Chinese truck in Africa — known for reliability, easy parts availability, and robust performance. Available in 4x2, 6x4, and 8x4 configurations with Cummins and Weichai engines.

Shacman (Shaanxi)

Shacman X3000 and X5000 series trucks feature MAN-based technology, Weichai engines, and FAST transmissions. Known for fuel efficiency, driver comfort, and durability in long-haul operations across Africa.

Dongfeng & Tongya Bodies

Dongfeng provides excellent light and medium-duty cargo trucks for urban and regional transport. Tongya Heavy Industry provides custom body configurations — flatbed, box, refrigerated, and crane bodies mounted on truck chassis from any brand.

Key Features

Wide Payload Range

From 2-ton light trucks for city delivery to 40-ton heavy-duty trucks for bulk transport. One source for all your cargo truck needs.

Multiple Body Options

Flatbed, box body, refrigerated (reefer), crane truck, or dump body. Each body type engineered for specific cargo and operating conditions.

African Spec Cooling

All trucks come with oversized A/C systems, heavy-duty radiators, and tropical cooling packages — essential for West and Central African climates.

Parts Availability

Sinotruk, Shacman, and Dongfeng have extensive parts distribution networks across Africa. Genuine parts available in Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, Tanzania, and more.
