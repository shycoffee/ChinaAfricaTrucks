Manufacturers

Huayu Trailers

Huayu specializes in heavy-duty lowbed trailers with capacities up to 100 tons. Their lowbeds feature reinforced stepped frames, heavy-duty spring suspensions, and hydraulic ramps. Huayu's lowbed trailers are the first choice for African mining and construction companies due to their proven durability under extreme loads.

Tongya Heavy Industry

Tongya offers custom lowbed configurations with multiple axle options and extendable decks. Their engineering team works with clients to design purpose-built trailers for specialized equipment transport needs.

Key Features

Ultra-Low Deck (0.9m)

Minimal deck height for transporting tall equipment with lower loading angles. Ideal for excavators, drilling rigs, and mining machinery.

Extendable Deck

Hydraulic extension system adds 2-4 meters of additional deck length, allowing flexible transport of oversized equipment without special permits.

Heavy-Duty Ramps

Hydraulic folding ramps rated for the same capacity as the trailer. Reinforced hinge points and anti-slip surface for safe loading.

Multi-Axle (3-5)

Multiple axle configurations distribute load evenly, reduce per-axle weight, and comply with axle load regulations in African countries.
