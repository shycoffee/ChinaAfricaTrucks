Manufacturers

Tongya Heavy Industry

Specialist in container trailers with robotic welding technology. Tongya's flatbed trailers feature precision-cut main beams, automated assembly lines, and strict QC processes. Their container trailers are designed for ISO container transport with 12-point locking systems and reinforced cross members.

Huayu Trailers

Heavy-duty flatbed trailers engineered specifically for African road conditions. Huayu uses thicker steel plate, reinforced suspension brackets, and heavy-duty spring packs. Their flatbeds are field-tested in West and East African markets with proven durability.

Key Features

Robotic Welding

All main seams are welded by robotic arms for consistent penetration, strength, and perfect bead appearance — no porosity or weak spots.

E-Coating Protection

Full chassis e-coating (electrophoretic paint) provides superior anti-corrosion protection, essential for tropical and coastal African environments.

Container Lock System

12 ISO-standard twist locks with flip-up mechanism allow fast container loading/unloading. Compatible with 20ft, 40ft, and 45ft containers.

Heavy-Duty Axles

FUWA or BPW axles with 12R22.5 tires rated for 60-ton payloads. Drum brakes with automatic slack adjusters for reliable stopping power.
