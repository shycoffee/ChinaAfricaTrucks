Manufacturers

Tongya Heavy Industry — Exon Series

Tongya is a leading manufacturer of aluminum powder tankers and liquid tankers. Their Exon Series features advanced aluminum welding technology, seamless compartment separation, and integrated safety systems. Ideal for fuel transport companies requiring lightweight, corrosion-resistant tankers.

Huayu Trailers

Huayu specializes in bulk powder and cement tankers with pneumatic unloading systems. Their tankers feature reinforced steel construction, efficient air compressor systems, and are designed for high-volume bulk material transport common in African infrastructure projects.

Key Features

Aluminum Construction

5083/5454 marine-grade aluminum alloy provides excellent corrosion resistance, lighter weight (more payload), and longer service life for fuel and chemical transport.

Pneumatic Unloading

Air compressor-powered unloading system for bulk powder and cement. 3-stage Roots blower for fast, efficient offloading at up to 1.2 bar pressure.

Multi-Compartment

1 to 6 compartments with internal baffles and independent discharge systems. Transport different grades of fuel or chemicals in a single trip.

Complete Safety Package

Emergency valves, vapor recovery, static grounding, fire extinguisher mounts, and anti-slip platforms. Fully compliant with international ADR standards.
